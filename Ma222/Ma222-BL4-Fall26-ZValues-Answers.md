# Answers: Z-Values

The mean is $\mu = 33$ and the standard deviation is $\sigma = 4$.

The z-value of a data value $x$ is

$$
z = \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma}
$$

**a) Fill in the x-values for each z-value on the number line.**

![](images/ZValues-answer.svg){width=80%}

$\mu = 33$ is at $z = 0$. Each step of one standard deviation adds or subtracts $4$:

- $33 + 4 = 37$ and $37 + 4 = 41$
- $33 - 4 = 29$ and $29 - 4 = 25$

### b) $x = 39$

![](images/ZValues-39.svg){width=80%}

**Visually:** $39$ is $6$ above the mean. Each small tick is $1$ and $4$ ticks make one standard deviation, so $6$ is $6/4 = 1.5$ standard deviations above the mean: $z = 1.5$.

**Calculate:**

$$
z = \frac{39 - 33}{4} = \frac{6}{4} = 1.5
$$
