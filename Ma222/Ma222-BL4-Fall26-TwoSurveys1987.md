# Two Surveys from 1987

## Shere Hite Survey

In 1987, researcher [Shere Hite](https://www.nytimes.com/2020/09/11/books/shere-hite-dead.html?unlocked_article_code=1.9VA.yZWg.JIpFAc-_gMP8&smid=url-share) published *Women and Love*, a study about American women and their relationships with men. Hite distributed approximately **100,000 questionnaires** and received about **4,500 completed questionnaires**.

The women who responded reported very high levels of dissatisfaction with their relationships. Among Hite's findings:

- **84%** said they were emotionally dissatisfied with their relationships.
- **95%** reported experiencing some form of emotional or psychological harassment from the men in their relationships.
- **70%** of women who had been married for five years or more reported having sex outside their marriages.
- Only **16%** said that arguments helped clear the air and led to better understanding.

Hite used these results to argue that dissatisfaction among American women in relationships was widespread.

**Questions**

a) Identify the population Hite was attempting to study.  
b) Identify the sample and give the sample size.  
c) Do you think the women who responded were representative of all American women?  
d) What percentage of the women who were sent questionnaires actually responded?

## Washington Post–ABC News Poll

After Hite's findings received widespread attention, the **Washington Post and ABC News** conducted a national public-opinion poll examining similar issues. They interviewed **1,505 adults nationwide by telephone**.

Their results painted a very different picture of American relationships:

- **97%** of respondents rated their romantic relationships as either **good or excellent**.
- About **two-thirds** of married people said their partners talked about their thoughts and feelings often enough to satisfy them.
- About **86%** of married respondents said that arguments tended to clear the air.
- More than **80%** of respondents said they felt they were equal partners in their relationships.
- About **75%** of respondents thought Hite's estimate that 70% of women married five years or more were having affairs was too high.

The Washington Post–ABC News poll had a reported margin of sampling error of approximately **±3 percentage points**.

**Questions**

a) Identify the population the Washington Post–ABC News poll was attempting to study.  
b) Identify the sample and give the sample size.  
c) Compare the results of the two surveys. For example, Hite reported that **84% of women were dissatisfied**, while the Washington Post–ABC News poll found that **97% of respondents rated their relationships as good or excellent**. Why might these surveys have produced such different results?
