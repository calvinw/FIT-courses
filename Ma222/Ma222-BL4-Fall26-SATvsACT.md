# SAT vs ACT

SAT scores have a mean of 1500 and a standard deviation of 240.

ACT scores have a mean of 21 and a standard deviation of 6.

a) Tim scored 1740 on the SAT. How many points above the SAT mean did he score?

b) Sally scored 30 on the ACT. How many points above the ACT mean did she score?

c) Find Tim's z-score.

d) Find Sally's z-score.

e) Who had the higher z-score?

f) So who did better?

g) What does a negative z-score mean?
