Problem 1: Variables of States

Suppose that the fifty states are the observational units. For each item, write yes, it is a variable or no, it is not a variable.

For each variable, report or make a reasonable guess for its value in your home state. For each item that is not a variable, briefly explain why.

a) The number of states that have a female governor

b) The percentage of the state’s residents who are over 65 years of age

c) The highest speed limit in the state

d) Whether the state’s name consists of one word

e) The average income of an adult resident of the state

f) The number of births per 1,000 residents of the state in 1999

g) The number of states that were settled before 1865

h) The average number of Congressional representatives per state

i) The number of Congressional representatives from the state
