Questions

  1. Show the first_name and last_name of every customer who placed an order between 2025-03-15 and 2025-03-21. List each customer only once.
  2. For every order of a product that costs more than $80, show the customer’s first_name, the product’s name, and the order_date.
  3. For each product that was ordered at least once, show its name and total_revenue, its price times the number of times it was ordered, sorted from highest total_revenue to lowest.
  4. Show the first_name and num_orders of every customer who placed more than one order.
  5. Show the first_name and last_name of every customer who has never ordered the Cotton T-Shirt.

Worked example. Show the name and price of every product Jane Smith ordered, sorted from lowest price to highest.

  1. In customers, Jane Smith has customer_id 2.
  2. In orders, customer_id 2 appears in orders 2, 4, and 6. Their product_ids are 1, 4, and 3.
  3. In products, look up 1, 4, and 3: Silk Blouse (89.99), Cotton T-Shirt (29.99), and Cashmere Sweater (149.99).
  4. Write the name and price of each and sort from the lowest price to the highest.
