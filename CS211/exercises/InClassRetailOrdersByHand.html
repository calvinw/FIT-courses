Retail Orders: Queries by Hand

Fall 26

Author

Calvin Williamson, Science and Math

The retail orders database has three tables. Each order is one customer buying one product. customer_id and product_id in orders point back to the customers and products tables.

Answer each question by hand, using only the tables below. Write your answer as a small table or a number. Later you will write a SQL query for each question and check your answers on the database.

The tables

customers

Level 1: one table

1. Which products cost less than $100? Give the name and price of each.

2. List all the products from most expensive to least expensive.

3. List the order number and date of every order Jane Smith placed.

4. How many orders were placed on or after March 15, 2025?

5. What are the average, highest, and lowest product prices?

Level 2: more than one table

6. For every order, give the order number, the first name of the customer, and the name of the product.

7. Who bought the Cashmere Sweater? Give their first and last names.

8. What is the total amount of money from all the orders?

Level 3: totals for each group

9. How much did each customer spend in total? List only customers who placed at least one order.

10. How many orders did each customer place? Include every customer, even one with no orders.

11. How many times was each product ordered? Include every product, even one that was never ordered.

Think about it

12. Which customer has never placed an order? How did you find out?

13. Which product has never been ordered? How did you find out?

14. If you paired every customer with every product, how many pairs would there be? Explain how you got your answer.
